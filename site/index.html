Residual Tones

Each of these ten directions is a line through the model's 3,584-dimensional activation space that separates one kind of sentence from neutral ones. Every direction is turned into a sound with the same fixed rule, so directions the model holds close together sound alike. Each card also plays the model itself, steered along that direction and asked to describe how someone alone in a quiet room feels.

Where the directions sit

How alike they are

Cosine similarity between the unit directions. The fear, negative emotion, arousal, numbness and body sensation directions form one cluster. The clean pain direction (S2) sits apart from all of them, with no similarity above 0.33.

The ten directions

How the sounds are made

  • Timbre. Each tone has 24 harmonics. Harmonic n is as loud as the direction's projection onto a fixed random unit vector rn. A random projection roughly keeps angles, so similar directions get similar harmonic profiles. Across the 45 pairs, the similarity of the harmonic profiles correlates with the true cosine at r = . The bars on each card are these 24 projections.
  • Roughness. Harmonics with a negative projection are doubled with a copy 9 cents sharp and panned right, so the sign pattern is heard as beating.
  • Pitch and brightness. The first principal component of the ten directions sets the fundamental from 98 to 196 Hz, and the second sets a low-pass cutoff. These two axes are the map above.
  • Pulse. The excess kurtosis of the components sets a tremolo from 0.5 to 6 Hz. A high value means a few dimensions carry most of the direction. Sadness and arousal pulse fastest.
  • Loudness. The log of the raw vector norm, within 9 dB. Sadness has by far the largest raw norm at 24.6, against 4.9 for clean pain.

What the voices are

  • The 4-bit MLX model gets the prompt . While it writes, the direction is added to the output of layer 8. Every direction is scaled to the same length, times the typical residual norm at that layer, so only the direction differs. The dose came from a short ladder on the pain and control directions. Lower doses changed little, and a dose of 1.0 on a direct question started to break the model into mixed Chinese and English.
  • The first of three samples is spoken by the macOS Samantha voice, decided before reading them. The other two are under each card.
  • The effect in the text is real but modest. The unsteered baseline already writes about heavy shoulders, because the prompt invites it. Pain, fear and negative emotion lean toward aching and isolation, and arousal and the bad world state direction lean toward calm. With three samples each, none of this is a measured difference.
  • The direction labelled control is built from the release's neutral Random sentences, so it is a real contrast direction and not random noise. It still shares 0.24 to 0.26 with fear and the bad world state.
  • The tones are a mapping chosen by a person. They show how the directions relate to each other. They are not a recording of anything the model experiences.
